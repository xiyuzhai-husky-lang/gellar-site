Coverage

CUDA. A structured subset: __global__ kernels with int and float scalars and arrays, __shared__ arrays, integer and float arithmetic, branches and loops, threadIdx/blockIdx/blockDim/gridDim, one- and two-dimensional launches, __syncthreads(), atomicAdd with the result unused on int and float, __shfl_down_sync over a full warp on int, and cuda::atomic_ref loads and stores of int with relaxed, acquire or release order at block, device or system scope. A kernel outside the subset fails to translate, with the line.

PTX. Scalar loads and stores, weak and strong with scope, atomic read-modify-writes, fence.sc, bar.sync and warp shuffles, on one GPU, through the generic proxy.

Outside. Aliasing, mixed-size accesses, asynchronous copies, clusters and multiple GPUs are outside the model. Floating-point contracts are about exact arithmetic; rounding is not bounded. Termination of a spin is not proved. The interleaving machine's replay of the PTX machine excludes shuffles and needs strict race freedom, so for release/acquire kernels Iris speaks about the sequentially consistent runs and the direct proof carries the PTX claim.

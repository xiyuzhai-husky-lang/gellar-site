Foundation

The foundation is a Lean formalization of a fragment of the PTX ISA memory consistency model (chapter 8): candidate executions with their events, the relations the ISA defines (morally strong, base causality order, observation, coherence, synchronizes-with through release/acquire patterns, barriers and fence.sc) and its axioms (Coherence, Fence-SC, Atomicity, No-Thin-Air, SC-per-location, Causality), each beside the ISA section it transcribes. A data race is the ISA's: a conflicting pair that is neither morally strong nor ordered by base causality order.

The formalization is checked against a litmus corpus by decision procedure against Dartagnan's verdicts, with the differences documented at the tests (one GPU, single-event atomics, no liveness).

Everything else is built on it and justified against it: the lockstep model in which barrier-synchronized kernels are proved, with its refinement theorem to the machine; the interleaving machine for kernels whose threads communicate, justified in both directions; and Iris, on iris-lean, as a proof method over the interleaving machine. Proofs are checked by Lean with its standard axioms only.
